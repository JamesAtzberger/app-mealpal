About

MealKin

James F. Atzberger
http://james.atzberger.org

This app was developed with the aim to help further engage young people and others in cooking healthy recipes. I hope the app will help with making healthy choices, learning to cook, and nutritious meal planning.

I hope you enjoy!

Acknowledgements:

The app was built using a combination of JavaScript tutorials, AI LLMs, and other web sources. This included using Anthropic Claude Sonet 5 to develop a Javascript tutorial for us to learn key functions and approaches through exercises and examples. We also learned concepts from the WC3 Tutorial Series https://www.w3schools.com/js/. We would also like to acknowledge help from Paul Atzberger. The data for our recipes builds on the USDA project MyPlate.food and their public domain database.
